Which Sealy models or years have fiberglass?

Newer hybrids and Cocoon models use a rayon and polyester barrier, not fiberglass. Sealy says the flame barrier in every Sealy mattress is a rayon and polyester blend. Cocoon by Sealy states its mattresses contain no fiberglass at all.

For all other foam-only models, Tempur Sealy says it uses a core-spun glass fiber technology. That glass sits inside a protective fire sock, never in the foam or the cover. The company says it stays fully enclosed during normal use, even if the cover is removed.

Older budget all-foam and mattress-in-a-box models are the ones shoppers most often ask about. Check your own law tag and Sealy's help pages for your exact model. For the Posturepedic line, see Does Sealy Posturepedic mattress have fiberglass?.

What fire barrier does Sealy use?

Sealy uses two barrier types, depending on the model. Sealy says its mattresses do not contain any form of fiberglass. The table below shows what the company discloses for each group.

Model groupFire barrierFiberglass?
Cocoon by SealyRayon and polyester blendNo
Hybrids (foam plus innersprings)Rayon and polyester blendNo
Other foam-only modelsCore-spun glass fiber in a fire sockGlass fiber, enclosed

Sealy says Cocoon and hybrid models are made without chemical flame retardants. The barrier helps them pass fire-resistance testing and meet federal, state, and local rules. Those rules include the federal mattress flammability standards at 16 C.F.R. parts 1632 and 1633. The goal is to reduce deaths and injuries from mattress fires.

How can you check your Sealy law tag?

Find the law tag under the mattress and read the fiber content line. Federal rules require a permanent label with the month and year of manufacture and the maker's location. Follow these steps.

  1. Lift the mattress and look along the bottom edge for a white or yellow tag.
  2. Read the fiber content list. Look for words like glass, fiberglass, or glass fiber.
  3. Note the month and year of manufacture so you know which model year you own.
  4. Compare the tag to Sealy's help pages for your model name.
  5. Keep a photo of the tag for your records.

Manufacturers must keep records on flame retardant treatments and flame resistant fibers used in mattress parts. If the tag is missing or unclear, contact Sealy support with your model name.

Should you unzip or wash the mattress cover?

No, do not unzip or wash the cover on most mattresses. Sealy says its core-spun glass fiber stays protected even if the cover is removed. But opening the cover still raises the risk of disturbing the fire sock inside.

Spot clean the surface with a damp cloth and mild soap instead. Use a waterproof mattress protector and wash that instead of the cover. If your cover is removable and washable, check the care label first. When in doubt, leave the cover zipped.

What should you do if you suspect fiberglass?

Stop using the mattress and keep the cover zipped until you confirm what is inside. Tempur Sealy says some makers use exposed, brittle fiberglass that can crumble and irritate skin. Sealy says its own glass fiber stays enclosed. Here is what to do.

  • Check the law tag for glass or fiber content.
  • Do not vacuum or shake the mattress if the cover is torn.
  • Bag and remove any loose material with gloves.
  • Wipe hard floors with a damp cloth, not a dry broom.
  • Contact Sealy support with your model name and tag photo.
  • See the Mattress Fiberglass Safety Hub for more cleanup tips.

How do you pick a fiberglass-free mattress?

Ask the brand for its fire barrier in writing before you buy. Sealy says its mattresses contain no form of fiberglass. Cocoon by Sealy repeats that claim for its own line.

Look for brands that name the barrier material, such as rayon and polyester. Ask whether the barrier sits in a sealed sock or loose in the cover. Check that the brand follows the federal standards at 16 C.F.R. parts 1632 and 1633. Tempur Sealy says it runs regular independent testing for safety and compliance. More FAQs about Sealy fiberglass (3)

QDoes every Sealy mattress use the same fire barrier?

No. Sealy says the barrier in every Sealy mattress is a rayon and polyester blend. For all other foam-only models, Tempur Sealy says it uses core-spun glass fiber inside a fire sock. Check your model's law tag to be sure.

QIs the glass fiber in Sealy foam mattresses dangerous?

Tempur Sealy says its core-spun glass stays fully enclosed and inaccessible during normal use. It also says the glass is never placed in the foam or the cover. The company contrasts this with exposed, brittle fiberglass used by some other makers.

QWhere can I learn more about mattress fire rules?

Sealy points readers to the Consumer Product Safety Commission at www.cpsc.gov. The CPSC lists two federal standards, 16 C.F.R. parts 1632 and 1633. Tempur Sealy says all its products comply with CPSC rules and California Proposition 65.
