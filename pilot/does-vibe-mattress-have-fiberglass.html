What fire barrier does Vibe Mattress use to meet federal rules?

All Vibe mattresses pass federal fire rules in U.S. labs without using fiberglass. Under federal CPSC mattress rules, beds must resist both smoldering cigarettes under 16 C.F.R. part 1632 and open flames under 16 C.F.R. part 1633. Vibe does not list the exact fiber blend of its inner fire barrier in the verified brand notes, so check the white law tag on your specific bed.

All polyurethane foams inside Vibe beds are CertiPUR-US certified. The outer covers meet Oeko-Tex Standard 100 rules, which screen fabrics for harmful dyes and finishes.

Do Amazon, Costco, or third-party Vibe Mattress models differ?

Vibe Mattress states its entire line is built without fiberglass. Still, third-party sellers may hold older stock in warehouses for months. Federal law requires every bed to carry a permanent label with the month and year it was made.

If you buy from a third-party site or clearance bin, check the white law tag right away. Confirm the make date and the fire-barrier materials before you sleep on the bed.

What exact language appears on a Vibe Mattress law tag?

We have not photographed every individual Vibe Mattress law tag in-house. However, federal law requires a permanent label on every mattress sold in the U.S.. The white law tag confirms the fire-barrier materials and the date the bed was made.

  1. Find the sewn-in white tag at the head or foot of the mattress.
  2. Read the material percentages to confirm there is no glass fiber listed.

Should you unzip or wash the Vibe Mattress mattress cover?

Do not unzip an inner fire-barrier sock or remove a non-washable cover. Vibe states its outer covers meet Oeko-Tex Standard 100 rules, and its beds are built to pass federal fire tests. Opening or damaging an inner fire barrier can ruin the fire protection and void your warranty. Always check the care label on your specific Vibe Essential Gel Memory Foam Mattress or hybrid model before cleaning.

Safe to do
  • Leave the outer cover zipped unless the care tag says it is washable.
  • Use a breathable, waterproof mattress protector to keep the bed clean.
  • Spot clean small spills with mild soap and a damp cloth.
Never do
  • Never unzip or cut open an inner fire-barrier sock.
  • Never machine wash or dry a mattress cover that is spot-clean only.
  • Never remove the permanent white law tag from the mattress.

Step 1: Blot spills gently with mild soap and cold water

Dab the spot lightly with a damp microfiber cloth and mild dish soap. Never soak the fabric or scrub through the top quilt into the fire-barrier layer underneath.

Step 2: Air-dry the mattress flat without unzipping the cover

Leave the cover zipped and point a fan across the damp spot until fully dry. Machine-washing or tumble-drying a structural mattress cover can shrink it and expose the inner barrier.

Step 3: Add a breathable waterproof mattress protector

Put a washable waterproof protector over the intact cover so sweat, spills, and dust mites never reach the mattress fabric in the first place.

How do you check an older or secondhand Vibe Mattress mattress?

If you are looking at a used or older Vibe bed, inspect the bed carefully before bringing it home:

  • Check the manufacture date: Federal rules require the month and year of manufacture on the permanent label.
  • Read the sewn-in fiber list: Confirm the white law tag lists the fire-barrier materials clearly.
  • Inspect the outer cover: Make sure the cover is intact with no tears, missing zippers, or loose fibers.
Current models are fiberglass-free. For general fiberglass cleanup steps on older secondhand beds, see our Mattress Fiberglass Safety Hub.
