What fire barrier does Kleinsleep use to meet federal rules?

Kleinsleep uses natural rayon and wool-rayon blends to pass federal fire rules. Under CPSC fire rules, every U.S. bed must pass a 30-minute open-flame test.

On the Willow Eco mattress, Kleinsleep pairs a rayon barrier with plant-based foam and an organic cotton cover. The Acacia Luxury Latex Hybrid uses a wool-rayon barrier and OEKO-TEX Standard 100 Talalay latex.

Do Amazon, Costco, or third-party Kleinsleep models differ?

Outside sellers and outlets sometimes carry older Kleinsleep beds not listed on the main site. Current models like the Spruce Pillow Top use natural barriers. Still, you shouldn't assume every outside listing is the same.

Always ask the seller for the exact model name and build date before you buy. You can also ask Kleinsleep to confirm the fire barrier in writing.

Should you unzip or wash the Kleinsleep mattress cover?

You shouldn't unzip or take off an inner fire-barrier sock on any Kleinsleep bed. Even without fiberglass, messing with the barrier can hurt the bed's fire safety.

Spot-clean the organic cotton cover with mild soap and water instead. If your bed has a zip-off outer cover, check the care tag first.

Safe to do
  • Spot-clean the organic cotton cover with mild soap and water.
  • Use a breathable mattress pad to keep spills off the cover.
  • Leave the sewn-in law tag on to protect your warranty.
Never do
  • Never unzip or take off an inner fire-barrier sock.
  • Never machine-wash a bed cover unless the care tag says it's safe.
  • Never cut off the law tag.

Step 1: Blot spills gently with mild soap and cold water

Dab stains lightly with a damp microfiber cloth and mild soap. Don't soak the cover or scrub down into the inner fire barrier.

Step 2: Air-dry the mattress flat without unzipping the cover

Keep the cover zipped and point a fan across the damp spot until it's dry. Washing a non-removable cover can shrink it and expose the inner sock.

Step 3: Add a breathable waterproof mattress protector

Put a washable waterproof protector over the cover on day one. It'll block sweat and spills before they ever reach the bed.

How do you check an older or secondhand Kleinsleep mattress?

You can check a used Kleinsleep bed by looking at its sewn-in law tag and outer cover. Follow these quick steps before you bring a used bed home:

  • Read the fiber list for rayon, wool, or cotton, and skip beds with glass fiber.
  • Check the build date to see if it's a newer model.
  • Check the outer cover for rips, tears, or missing zipper pulls.
  • Ask the seller for the old receipt or model name.

Mattress fiberglass terms explained in plain English

Confused by terms like “fire sock,” “silica,” “URN,” or “CertiPUR-US” while checking your Kleinsleep bed? Here's what each safety term on a tag or product page actually means.

Inner fire sock (FR sock)

It's a thin, stretchy mesh sleeve wrapped tightly around the foam core under the outer cover. It slows open flames, and it can shed glass shards if you unzip or tear the cover.

Assembly zipper vs. washable cover

A zipper on the bottom usually exists for factory assembly, not washing. Don't unzip it unless the care tag clearly says the outer cover is washable.

Glass fiber and core-spun glass

These are common tag names for fiberglass. Treat glass fiber, fiberglass, glass wool, core-spun glass, and glass yarn as the same woven glass material.

Silica-treated rayon vs. fiberglass

Silica-infused rayon chars safely like ash and doesn't shed sharp glass needles. Seeing silica-treated rayon alone isn't the same as woven fiberglass.

CertiPUR-US doesn't mean fiberglass-free

CertiPUR-US only tests the inner foam for VOCs and chemicals, never the fire sock. Only finished-product GOTS or GOLS organic standards ban fiberglass socks.

6-sided encasement vs. fitted protector

A fitted protector leaves the bottom seam open. Use a 6-sided zippered waterproof encasement if you need to seal a suspect bed completely.

URN and manufacture date

The Uniform Registry Number (like CA-XXXXX) and build date identify the factory and year. You'll need that date to check older pre-transition stock.

16 CFR Part 1632 vs. 1633

Every U.S. bed must pass both federal fire tests. Part 1632 tests smoldering cigarettes, while Part 1633 tests a 30-minute open flame.

QDoes the Kleinsleep Willow Eco mattress have fiberglass?

No, the Kleinsleep Willow Eco doesn't have fiberglass or harsh chemical barriers. It uses a natural rayon fire barrier, plant-based foam, and an organic cotton cover.

QWhat fire barrier is in the Kleinsleep Acacia mattress?

The Kleinsleep Acacia Luxury and Acacia Firm models use a wool and rayon fire barrier. They also have a 100% organic cotton cover and Talalay latex.

QHas Kleinsleep ever had a mattress recall?

No, Kleinsleep has zero recalls in U.S. CPSC records as of Nov 23, 2026. A direct CPSC Recall API check also showed zero recalls.

QAre Kleinsleep Alder and Waverly models fiberglass-free?

Yes, Kleinsleep Alder and Waverly models use a natural fire barrier, plant-based foams, and an organic cotton cover. Still, they don't have full cert numbers posted online.

QCan I remove the law tag on my Kleinsleep mattress?

You shouldn't cut off the law tag on your Kleinsleep bed. The tag shows the maker, date, and fiber blend, and taking it off can hurt a warranty claim.

QHow do Kleinsleep mattresses pass federal fire rules without fiberglass?

Kleinsleep uses natural rayon and wool-rayon barriers to pass the 30-minute open-flame test under 16 C.F.R. part 1633. These materials char instead of melting, so they don't need glass fibers.
