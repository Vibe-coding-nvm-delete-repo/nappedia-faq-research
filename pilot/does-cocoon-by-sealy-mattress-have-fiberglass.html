What fire barrier does Cocoon by Sealy use to meet federal rules?

Every Cocoon by Sealy mattress uses a rayon and polyester blend as its flame barrier. This non-toxic fire sock replaces glass fibers to keep the bed safe.

Federal rules from the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission require mattresses to reduce fire deaths and injuries. Under 16 C.F.R. part 1632, a mattress must resist smoldering ignition from a lighted cigarette. Under 16 C.F.R. part 1633, the mattress must pass a 30-minute open-flame test that measures fire size. Cocoon by Sealy meets 16 CFR Part 1633 using its rayon and polyester fire barrier.

Do Amazon, Costco, or third-party Cocoon by Sealy models differ?

Cocoon by Sealy states that its mattresses do not contain any form of fiberglass. Current Chill Memory Foam and Chill Hybrid beds on CocoonBySealy.com are confirmed 100% fiberglass-free.

Even so, buyers should always read their law tag when purchasing from any third-party seller. Checking the tag ensures you verify the exact materials inside your specific mattress.

What exact language appears on a Cocoon by Sealy law tag?

Federal law requires every mattress to carry a permanent manufacture label showing the month, year, and location of manufacture. While we have not photographed every individual tag in-house, shoppers should look for the rayon and polyester blend listed on the law tag.

  1. Locate the white sewn-in law tag along the bottom or side seam of your mattress.
  2. Check the month and year of manufacture printed on the permanent label.
  3. Read the fiber content list to confirm it lists rayon and polyester rather than glass fiber.
  4. Leave the tag attached so you keep a permanent record of your bed's materials.

Should you unzip or wash the Cocoon by Sealy mattress cover?

Never unzip or wash the outer cover of your Cocoon by Sealy mattress. Even though the bed uses a non-toxic rayon and polyester fire sock instead of fiberglass, the outer cover is not meant for machine washing. Keeping the cover zipped protects the inner fire barrier and keeps the mattress layers intact.

Safe to do
  • Spot-clean small spills gently on the surface without removing the cover.
  • Use a breathable mattress protector to keep the PCM cooling cover clean.
  • Read the sewn-in law tag to confirm the manufacture date and materials.
Never do
  • Never unzip the outer cover of your Cocoon by Sealy mattress.
  • Never machine-wash or dry-clean the mattress cover.
  • Never remove or cut off the permanent manufacture law tag.

Step 1: Blot spills gently with mild soap and cold water

Dab the spot lightly with a damp microfiber cloth and mild dish soap. Never soak the fabric or scrub through the top quilt into the fire-barrier layer underneath.

Step 2: Air-dry the mattress flat without unzipping the cover

Leave the cover zipped and point a fan across the damp spot until fully dry. Machine-washing or tumble-drying a structural mattress cover can shrink it and expose the inner barrier.

Step 3: Add a breathable waterproof mattress protector

Put a washable waterproof protector over the intact cover so sweat, spills, and dust mites never reach the mattress fabric in the first place.

How do you check an older or secondhand Cocoon by Sealy mattress?

If you are buying a secondhand Cocoon by Sealy bed, use these quick checks before bringing it inside:

  • Check the manufacture date: Every mattress must have a permanent label with the month and year it was made.
  • Read the sewn-in fiber list: Confirm the law tag lists a rayon and polyester blend with no glass fiber.
  • Inspect the outer cover: Make sure the cover is intact and has not been unzipped or washed.
Current models are fiberglass-free. For general fiberglass cleanup steps on older secondhand beds, see our Mattress Fiberglass Safety Hub.
