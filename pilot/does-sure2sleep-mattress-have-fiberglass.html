What fire barrier does Sure2Sleep use to meet federal rules?

Sure2Sleep uses a fiberglass-free fire sock on The Monterey Mattress. The brand does not publish the exact cover fabric or full layer stack for this bed. Some other brands use fiberglass socks to pass fire tests. Sure2Sleep avoids fiberglass for customer health.

All mattresses sold in the U.S. must pass federal fire rules. The CPSC mattress rules include 16 C.F.R. part 1632 for smoldering cigarettes. They also include 16 C.F.R. part 1633 for a 30-minute open-flame test.

Do Amazon, Costco, or third-party Sure2Sleep models differ?

Sure2Sleep states its products use American craftsmanship with no overseas shortcuts. We do not have verified facts on special Amazon or Costco builds. Always check the sewn-in law tag on any third-party bed. Federal rules require every mattress to show its manufacture date and plant location.

What exact language appears on a Sure2Sleep law tag?

We have not photographed every individual Sure2Sleep law tag in-house. Under federal rule 16 C.F.R. part 1632, every mattress must have a permanent sewn-in label. That label must list the month, year, and plant location.

Follow these four steps to inspect your tag: 1. Find the white law tag sewn into the mattress seam. 2. Check the month and year of manufacture. 3. Read the fiber list for any mention of glass fiber or fiberglass. 4. Confirm the manufacturing plant location matches U.S. production.

Should you unzip or wash the Sure2Sleep mattress cover?

Do not unzip or remove an inner fire-barrier sock on any mattress. Sure2Sleep wraps The Monterey Mattress in a fiberglass-free fire sock. Even without fiberglass, disturbing a fire sock can harm fire protection. Check our Mattress Fiberglass Safety Hub for safe care tips.

Safe to do
  • Spot-clean the outer cover with mild soap and water.
  • Use a washable mattress protector to keep the surface clean.
  • Leave the inner fire-barrier sock zipped and intact.
Never do
  • Never unzip or remove the inner fire-barrier sock.
  • Never machine-wash a non-removable mattress cover.
  • Never cut or tear the sewn-in federal law tag.

Step 1: Blot spills gently with mild soap and cold water

Dab the spot lightly with a damp microfiber cloth and mild dish soap. Never soak the fabric or scrub through the top quilt into the fire-barrier layer underneath.

Step 2: Air-dry the mattress flat without unzipping the cover

Leave the cover zipped and point a fan across the damp spot until fully dry. Machine-washing or tumble-drying a structural mattress cover can shrink it and expose the inner barrier.

Step 3: Add a breathable waterproof mattress protector

Put a washable waterproof protector over the intact cover so sweat, spills, and dust mites never reach the mattress fabric in the first place.

How do you check an older or secondhand Sure2Sleep mattress?

If you buy a used Sure2Sleep bed, inspect it before bringing it inside. - Check the manufacture date: Look for the month and year on the permanent label. - Read the fiber list: Make sure no glass fiber is listed on the tag. - Inspect the outer cover: Check for tears, rips, or open seams.

Current models are fiberglass-free. For general fiberglass cleanup steps on older secondhand beds, see our Mattress Fiberglass Safety Hub.
