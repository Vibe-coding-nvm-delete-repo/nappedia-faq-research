Which Novaform models or years have fiberglass?

Current Novaform models are fiberglass-free, but older ones may not be. Novaform says all its mattresses and toppers have zero fiberglass. This covers lines like ComfortGrande, ComfortGrande Plus, Serafina Pearl, and SoFresh. But a third-party article says Novaform had fiberglass in the past. So models made years ago could still have it. The same article says the newer sub-brand Nue by Novaform reportedly has no fiberglass. If you have an older Novaform, check the law tag and the date it was made. For a wider comparison, see Does Sealy mattress have fiberglass?.

What fire barrier does Novaform use?

Novaform uses safer fire retardant alternatives instead of fiberglass. The company says its mattresses use safer fire retardant alternatives. But Novaform does not name the exact fire-barrier material on its pages. Here is a simple comparison:

FeatureOlder Novaform (possible)Current Novaform
FiberglassMay be presentAbsolutely zero
Fire barrierUnknown, possibly fiberglassSafer alternatives, not named
CertificationsNot specifiedCertiPUR-US certified foams

All Novaform foams are CertiPUR-US certified. This means no phthalates, no heavy metals, low VOCs, and no ozone depleters. Novaform makes its memory foam products in the USA with imported covers.

How can you check your Novaform law tag?

Check the law tag for the date it was made and any fiberglass warnings. Federal law requires every mattress to have a permanent label. It must show the month and year it was made and the maker's location. Follow these steps:

  1. Find the law tag. It is usually sewn into the mattress edge or under the cover.
  2. Look for the date it was made (month and year).
  3. Check for any mention of fiberglass or glass fibers.
  4. If the tag mentions a chemical flame retardant, look for the letter "T".
  5. Note the maker's location and model name.
  6. If you are unsure, contact Novaform customer care at 1-888-999-0499 or customercare@novaformcomfort.com.

Keep the law tag safe. Novaform warranty claims require all law tags plus proof of purchase.

Should you unzip or wash the mattress cover?

No, do not unzip or remove the mattress cover. The fiberglass problem starts when someone unzips and removes the outer cover. This releases tiny glass fibers. These fibers can spread through your whole home. Even if your Novaform has no fiberglass, removing the cover can damage the mattress. Also, using the mattress right on the floor can damage it and will void the limited warranty. So keep the cover on and spot clean only. If you need to wash bedding, wash the sheets and mattress pad, not the mattress cover.

What should you do if you suspect fiberglass?

Stop using the mattress and do not disturb the cover if you suspect fiberglass. Here are practical steps:

  • Do not unzip or remove the cover.
  • Vacuum the area with a HEPA filter to pick up stray fibers.
  • Wipe down hard surfaces with a damp cloth.
  • Wash bedding separately on a gentle cycle.
  • Contact Novaform customer care at 1-888-999-0499 or customercare@novaformcomfort.com.
  • Check your law tag for the date it was made to see if your model is older.
  • If the mattress is old and you are unsure, consider replacing it with a fiberglass-free model.

How do you pick a fiberglass-free mattress?

Look for clear fiberglass-free claims and check the law tag. Choose a brand that says its mattresses have no fiberglass, like current Novaform. Avoid mattresses under $500. Fiberglass is one of the cheapest ways to meet federal fire safety standards. Check that the mattress passes 16 CFR 1633, the open-flame test. Also look for CertiPUR-US certified foams. Ask the seller for the law tag details before you buy. For other brands, see Does Avocado mattress have fiberglass?.

More FAQs about Novaform fiberglass (3)

QIs fiberglass used in Novaform mattresses to meet fire safety rules?

No, current Novaform mattresses do not use fiberglass for fire safety. Novaform says it uses safer fire retardant alternatives instead. But the company does not name the exact material it uses.

QDo all Novaform models, including those sold at Costco, have no fiberglass?

Novaform's current fiberglass-free claim covers all its mattresses and toppers, not just one model line. This includes models sold at Costco like ComfortGrande and Serafina Pearl. But older models may still have fiberglass, according to a third-party article.

QWhat does the law tag tell me about fiberglass in my Novaform?

The law tag shows the date it was made and the maker's location. It may also mention flame retardant treatments or fibers. If you see fiberglass listed, contact Novaform customer care. Keep the tag for warranty claims.
