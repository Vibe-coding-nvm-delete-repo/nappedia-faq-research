Which Novaform models or years have fiberglass?

Current Novaform models are fiberglass-free, but older stock may not be. Novaform says its mattresses and toppers contain absolutely zero fiberglass. The same claim appears on the company's Our Story page. FXI, the maker of Novaform, confirms all active Novaform beds have zero fiberglass.

One report says Novaform historically contained fiberglass. That is why year and model checks still matter. The newer sub-brand Nue by Novaform reportedly does not contain fiberglass either.

What fire barrier does Novaform use instead of fiberglass?

Novaform meets federal fire rules with a woven fire liner, not glass fiber. To pass federal fire rules, FXI uses a woven fire liner under the cover instead of glass fiber. Novaform says its fiberglass-free products use safer fire retardant alternatives.

Federal rule 16 C.F.R. part 1633 tests mattress flammability against an open flame with a 30-minute test. A fire barrier must sit between the foam and the outside world to pass that test.

Novaform foams also carry CertiPUR-US testing. Each Novaform foam layer has CertiPUR-US testing for low VOCs under 0.5 ppm and zero heavy metals or banned chemicals. Nappedia states Novaform beds have zero fiberglass and use CertiPUR-US foam.

The liner stays inside the cover. Do not remove it, because it is part of how the bed passes the fire test.

What exact language appears on a Novaform law tag?

A Novaform law tag should not list fiberglass as a fire barrier material. Fiberglass in mattresses is typically woven into a sock-like layer just beneath the outer cover as a fire barrier. On a fiberglass-free Novaform, that layer is a woven liner instead.

Use this checklist before you buy or keep a bed:

  1. Find the law tag sewn onto the mattress. It is required for warranty claims.
  2. Read the fire barrier or filling lines. Look for words like "fiberglass," "glass fiber," or "fiber glass."
  3. Check the manufacture date. Nappedia advises checking 2023 law tags against Recall #23-793.
  4. Keep the tag and your receipt. Novaform warranty claims require all law tags along with proof of purchase or packing slip.

No fiberglass wording is the goal. If the tag is missing or unclear, ask the seller before you buy.

Are Costco Novaform mattresses fiberglass-free?

Yes, current Costco Novaform beds are fiberglass-free, but check the tag on older stock. Novaform states its mattresses and toppers contain absolutely zero fiberglass. FXI confirms all active Novaform beds have zero fiberglass.

Costco shoppers should still look closely at two models. The recalled beds were sold exclusively at Costco (Northwest and Bay Area stores and Costco.com) from January to June 2023. Those beds were the ComfortGrande 14" and DreamAway 8". That recall was about mold, not fiberglass.

Why the tag matters: older warehouse stock can sit for months. A law tag check takes one minute and confirms the build.

Check the box and the tag. If you bought a 2023 Costco bed, compare the item number to the recall list.

How does CPSC Recall #23-793 relate to Novaform fiberglass concerns?

Recall #23-793 is a mold recall, not a fiberglass recall. Novaform has one major CPSC recall: Recall #23-793 from September 2023 covering about 48,000 ComfortGrande 14" and DreamAway 8" beds. Recall #23-793 was issued on September 21, 2023 by CPSC and FXI for mold risk.

The recall followed over 500 mold reports after water got onto foam at FXI's San Bernardino, California plant. Recalled item numbers include ComfortGrande 14" King #1413200, Cal King #1413201, Queen #1413202, Full #1413203, Twin #1413204, and DreamAway 8" Twin #1698562 and Full #1698564. Affected owners can call FXI at 888-886-2057 for a free replacement or full refund.

Novaform's legal record centers on CPSC Recall #23-793 and follow-up mold investigations rather than a fiberglass class action. Because Novaform uses fiberglass-free liners, it has avoided the fiberglass lawsuits seen by some budget boxed brands.

Mold and fiberglass are separate issues. Do not mix them up when you research a bed.

Should you unzip or wash a Novaform mattress cover?

No, do not unzip or wash a Novaform mattress cover. Nappedia warns never to unzip or wash the outer cover, as that can harm the fire liner and void the warranty. Fiberglass release problems begin when someone unzips and removes the outer cover.

Spot clean the surface with a damp cloth and mild soap. Use a mattress protector to keep the cover clean. Air the room if the bed has a new-foam smell, which fades in 24 to 72 hours with an open window.

Leave the inner sock alone. The fire barrier is not a washable part.

Safe to do

  • Never unzip or remove the outer cover.
  • Never wash the cover in a machine or dry it in a dryer.
  • Never cut or remove the inner fire-barrier liner.

Do Novaform mattress toppers and pillows contain fiberglass?

Novaform toppers are fiberglass-free, and the federal fire rule treats them differently from full mattresses. Novaform states its mattresses and toppers contain absolutely zero fiberglass. The same fiberglass-free claim for mattresses and toppers appears on Novaform's Our Story page.

Federal rule 16 C.F.R. part 1633 covers mattress flammability when exposed to an open flame and prescribes a 30-minute test. That rule targets full mattresses and mattress sets, not every pillow or thin topper. So a topper may not need the same fire barrier sock.

To check a topper or pillow, read the sewn-in tag and the online listing. Look for fiberglass wording and confirm the brand name. Nue by Novaform reportedly does not contain fiberglass either.

Read the tag on toppers too. A quick check beats guessing.
