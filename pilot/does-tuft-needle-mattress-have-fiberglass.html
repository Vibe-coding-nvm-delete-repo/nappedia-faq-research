What fire barrier does Tuft & Needle use to meet federal rules?

Tuft & Needle uses a fire-resistant barrier sewn into the top panel. This barrier is made from rayon fibers treated with ammonium phosphate. You can read more on the Tuft & Needle help page.

Federal rules require all U.S. mattresses to pass strict fire tests. Under CPSC mattress rules, beds must resist smoldering cigarettes under 16 C.F.R. part 1632. They must also pass a 30-minute open-flame test under 16 C.F.R. part 1633. Tuft & Needle meets these rules using its rayon and ammonium phosphate barrier instead of fiberglass.

Do Amazon, Costco, or third-party Tuft & Needle models differ?

Tuft & Needle states that its mattresses are free from fiberglass in any component. That statement applies to the brand's mattress line. However, buying direct from Tuft & Needle gives you a 365-night sleep trial on mattresses.

Third-party sellers may offer different return rules or older boxed stock. Always check the sewn-in law tag when a bed arrives from any store. Under federal law, every mattress must carry a permanent label with its manufacture date and factory location.

What exact language appears on a Tuft & Needle law tag?

Every U.S. mattress must have a permanent law tag under 16 C.F.R. part 1632. This tag lists the month and year the bed was made, plus the factory location. Note that we have not photographed every individual Tuft & Needle tag in-house.

Follow these 4 steps to check your tag: 1. Find the white sewn-in tag at the foot or side seam. 2. Read the fiber content list for rayon, polyester, or cotton. 3. Check the manufacture month and year. 4. Look for the letter T if it is a treated mattress pad.

Should you unzip or wash the Tuft & Needle mattress cover?

Even though Tuft & Needle does not use fiberglass, you should still leave the cover alone. The fire barrier is sewn into the top panel. Pulling or washing the cover can damage that safety layer.

Use a washable mattress protector on top instead. Spot-clean small spills with mild soap and cold water.

Safe to do
  • Put a washable waterproof protector over the mattress.
  • Spot-clean small stains with mild soap and damp cloth.
  • Vacuum the top surface gently with a soft brush.
Never do
  • Never unzip or remove the outer mattress cover.
  • Never machine-wash or dry-clean the mattress cover.
  • Never cut or pull at the sewn-in fire barrier.

Step 1: Blot spills gently with mild soap and cold water

Dab the spot lightly with a damp microfiber cloth and mild dish soap. Never soak the fabric or scrub through the top quilt into the fire-barrier layer underneath.

Step 2: Air-dry the mattress flat without unzipping the cover

Leave the cover zipped and point a fan across the damp spot until fully dry. Machine-washing or tumble-drying a structural mattress cover can shrink it and expose the inner barrier.

Step 3: Add a breathable waterproof mattress protector

Put a washable waterproof protector over the intact cover so sweat, spills, and dust mites never reach the mattress fabric in the first place.

How do you check an older or secondhand Tuft & Needle mattress?

If you buy a used Tuft & Needle bed, inspect it before bringing it inside. Use these quick checks: * Check the manufacture date on the permanent law tag. * Read the fiber list to confirm rayon or foam materials, not glass fiber. * Inspect the outer cover for rips, tears, or missing seams. * Check for a clean surface with no loose shiny fibers.

Current models are fiberglass-free. For general fiberglass cleanup steps on older secondhand beds, see our Mattress Fiberglass Safety Hub.

QDoes the Tuft & Needle Original mattress have fiberglass?

No, the Tuft & Needle Original does not have fiberglass. The brand states all of its mattresses are free from fiberglass in every part.

QWhat does Tuft & Needle use instead of fiberglass?

Tuft & Needle uses a fire-resistant barrier sewn into the top panel. It is made from rayon fibers treated with ammonium phosphate.

QAre Tuft & Needle foams safe from harmful chemicals?

Yes, Tuft & Needle foams are CertiPUR-US certified to be free from harmful chemicals and gasses. Its materials are also Greenguard Gold certified and screened for over 10,000 VOCs.

QHow long is the Tuft & Needle mattress trial?

Tuft & Needle mattresses include a 365-night risk-free trial. Other eligible products from the brand come with a 100-night trial.

QDo Tuft & Needle hybrid mattresses have fiberglass?

No, Tuft & Needle hybrid beds are also free from fiberglass. They pair Adaptive foam with pocketed steel coils instead of all-foam layers.

QHow can I check the age of a Tuft & Needle mattress?

Look at the permanent law tag sewn onto the bed. Under 16 C.F.R. part 1632, it must show the month and year of manufacture.
