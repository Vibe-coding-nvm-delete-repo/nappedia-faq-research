What fire barrier does Puffy use to meet federal rules?

Puffy uses a fiberglass-free fire barrier, though the brand does not list one exact fiber blend for every bed online. Federal rules require every mattress sold in the U.S. to pass strict fire tests. Puffy meets these rules without using fiberglass in its fire barriers. Under CPSC mattress flammability rules, beds must pass 16 C.F.R. part 1632 for smoldering cigarettes and 16 C.F.R. part 1633 for open flames. Puffy Monarch and Legacy use natural wool and Talalay latex, which help slow down flames naturally. Other Puffy models use safe, non-fiberglass flame barriers to pass the 30-minute open-flame test.

Do Amazon, Costco, or third-party Puffy models differ?

Puffy sells its beds directly on the Puffy website and through select retail partners. Third-party or clearance stock can sometimes differ from the newest direct models. Before you buy from an outside seller, check the listing details and ask for the manufacture date. You can also read the Puffy warranty page to see how coverage works for the original owner.

What exact language appears on a Puffy law tag?

On a Puffy law tag, look for Polyurethane Foam / Gel Foam or Wool / Natural Latex and make sure glass fiber or fiberglass is not listed. Federal law requires a permanent label on every mattress. We have not photographed every individual Puffy tag in-house, so you should always inspect your own tag. Look for the white sewn-in law tag at the head or foot of the bed.

  1. Find the permanent label showing the month and year of manufacture.
  2. Read the 'All New Material' section to see the exact fiber percentages.
  3. Check for terms like 'glass fiber' or 'fiberglass' (which should not appear on a fiberglass-free bed).
  4. Look for the letter 'T' if the pad was treated with a chemical flame retardant.

Should you unzip or wash the Puffy mattress cover?

Many Puffy beds have a zip-off outer cover, but you must handle it with care. Never unzip or remove an inner fire-barrier sock under the main cover. Disturbing the inner fire layer can damage the bed and void your Puffy mattress warranty. If you only want to clean the outer fabric, follow the care tag and keep the inner layers sealed.

Safe to do
  • Spot clean the outer cover with mild soap and cold water.
  • Use a waterproof mattress protector to keep spills off the bed.
  • Read the permanent law tag for exact care rules before washing.
Never do
  • Never unzip or cut into the inner fire-barrier sock.
  • Never put the entire mattress or foam core in a washing machine.
  • Never use harsh bleach or high heat on the outer cover.

Step 1: Blot spills gently with mild soap and cold water

Dab the spot lightly with a damp microfiber cloth and mild dish soap. Never soak the fabric or scrub through the top quilt into the fire-barrier layer underneath.

Step 2: Air-dry the mattress flat without unzipping the cover

Leave the cover zipped and point a fan across the damp spot until fully dry. Machine-washing or tumble-drying a structural mattress cover can shrink it and expose the inner barrier.

Step 3: Add a breathable waterproof mattress protector

Put a washable waterproof protector over the intact cover so sweat, spills, and dust mites never reach the mattress fabric in the first place.

How do you check an older or secondhand Puffy mattress?

Buying a used mattress takes extra care because you may not know its full history. Check three things before bringing a used Puffy home:

  • Manufacture date: Read the permanent law tag for the month and year it was made.
  • Fiber list: Confirm the tag lists foam, wool, latex, or polyester, and not glass fiber.
  • Outer cover: Make sure the cover is intact with no rips, tears, or missing inner layers.
Current models are fiberglass-free. For general fiberglass cleanup steps on older secondhand beds, see our Mattress Fiberglass Safety Hub.

Mattress fiberglass terms explained in plain English

Confused by terms like “fire sock,” “silica,” “URN,” or “CertiPUR-US” while checking your Puffy mattress? Here is what each safety term on a mattress tag or product page actually means.

Inner Fire Sock (FR Sock)

A thin, stretchy mesh sleeve pulled tightly around the foam core underneath the outer cover. It slows open flames, and can shed tiny glass shards if the outer cover is unzipped or torn.

Assembly Zipper vs. Washable Cover

A zipper on the bottom of a mattress usually exists only for factory assembly, not washing. Never unzip it unless the care tag clearly says the outer cover is removable and washable.

Glass Fiber / Core-Spun Glass

Common U.S. law-tag names for fiberglass. Treat 'glass fiber,' 'fiberglass,' 'glass wool,' 'core-spun glass,' and 'textured glass yarn' as the same woven glass barrier.

Silica-Treated Rayon vs. Fiberglass

Silica-infused rayon or viscose chars safely like ash and does not shed sharp glass needles. Seeing 'silica-treated rayon' alone is not the same as woven glass fiber.

CertiPUR-US Does Not Mean Fiberglass-Free

CertiPUR-US only tests the foam inside the bed for VOCs and chemicals, never the outer cover or fire sock. Only finished-product GOTS or GOLS organic standards ban fiberglass socks.

6-Sided Encasement vs. Fitted Protector

A fitted protector only covers the top and sides, leaving the bottom seam open. Use a 6-sided zippered waterproof encasement if you want to seal a suspect mattress completely.

URN & Manufacture Date

The Uniform Registry Number (like CA-XXXXX) and build date on the white law tag identify the exact factory and year. Use that date to check pre-transition or older stock.

16 CFR Part 1632 vs. 1633

Two federal fire rules every U.S. bed must pass: 1632 tests smoldering cigarettes, while 1633 tests a 30-minute open flame (which pushed budget foam brands to use fire socks).

QDoes the Puffy Cloud mattress have fiberglass?

No, the Puffy Cloud does not have fiberglass. It is an all-foam bed made with slow-sinking memory foam. Users report contouring comfort with firm support and cooler nights.

QIs the Puffy Lux Hybrid fiberglass-free?

Yes, the Puffy Lux Hybrid is fiberglass-free. It pairs gel foam with Contour-Adapt steel coils for easier turning and better airflow. Users report less lower-back and hip pain.

QWhat does Puffy use instead of fiberglass for fire safety?

Puffy uses non-fiberglass fire barriers to pass federal 16 C.F.R. part 1632 and part 1633 tests. Models like the Monarch and Legacy also use natural wool and Talalay latex.

QCan I wash my Puffy mattress cover?

You should only clean the outer cover as directed on the care label. Never unzip or remove the inner fire-barrier layer underneath it. Doing so can damage the bed and affect your warranty.

QHow do I know when my Puffy mattress was made?

Check the permanent white law tag sewn into the mattress. Federal rules require every mattress to list the month and year of manufacture and the maker's location.

QDoes Puffy offer a warranty on its mattresses?

Yes, Puffy guarantees its mattresses will be free from defects in materials and workmanship. This coverage lasts for as long as the original owner owns the mattress.
