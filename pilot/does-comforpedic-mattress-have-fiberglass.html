What fire barrier does ComforPedic use to meet federal rules?

ComforPedic doesn't post its fire-barrier fiber mix on its main pages. Every bed sold in the U.S. must pass federal fire rules under CPSC mattress standards. Those rules test lit cigarettes under 16 CFR Part 1632 and open flames under 16 CFR Part 1633. Brands pass those tests with wool, rayon, poly, or glass fiber, so check your bed's tag to be sure.

Do Amazon, Costco, or third-party ComforPedic models differ?

Yes, third-party ComforPedic listings can differ from store to store. For example, the Wayfair Medium listing says it is fiberglass-free, while Home Depot and 12-inch SKUs don't show that same text. Since comforpedic.com is offline and parent-company facts aren't on store pages, don't rely on one store's page for every SKU. If you're unsure, message Beautyrest customer support with the exact model number before you order.

What exact language appears on a ComforPedic law tag?

We haven't seen every ComforPedic law tag in person, and federal law doesn't make tags list fire-barrier fibers. Under CPSC rules, each bed must have a fixed tag showing the build month, build year, and plant. You should still keep that white law tag on your bed so the store can check your model for a claim.

  1. Find the white sewn-in law tag on the foot or side seam.
  2. Check the build month and year on the tag.
  3. Read the fill and cover lines for glass fiber or silica.
  4. Take a clear photo of the tag before you put on a cover.

Should you unzip or wash the ComforPedic mattress cover?

No, you shouldn't unzip or take off a ComforPedic cover unless the care tag says you can wash it. ComforPedic covers use stretch cloth made to stay on over the foam. If a bed has an inner fire sock, opening the outer cover can let fibers out. Spot-clean spills with mild soap and use a thin bed cover instead.

Safe to do
  • Spot-clean spills with mild soap and cold water.
  • Use a waterproof bed cover over the mattress.
  • Keep the white law tag on for claim checks.
Never do
  • Never unzip or take off an inner fire-barrier sock.
  • Never machine-wash or dry a non-washable outer cover.
  • Never cut off the white law tag.

Step 1: Blot spills gently with mild soap and cold water

Dab stains lightly with a damp microfiber cloth and mild soap. Don't soak the cover or scrub down into the inner fire barrier.

Step 2: Air-dry the mattress flat without unzipping the cover

Keep the cover zipped and point a fan across the damp spot until it's dry. Washing a non-removable cover can shrink it and expose the inner sock.

Step 3: Add a breathable waterproof mattress protector

Put a washable waterproof protector over the cover on day one. It'll block sweat and spills before they ever reach the bed.

How do you check an older or secondhand ComforPedic mattress?

You can check an older or used ComforPedic bed in two minutes by looking at its tag and cover. Since comforpedic.com is offline, hands-on checks matter most when you buy used.

  • Check the white law tag for the build month and year.
  • Read each fiber line on the tag to see if glass fiber is listed.
  • Check the stretch outer cover for rips, loose seams, or missing zips.
  • Ask the seller for the exact model name, like 12-inch Choose Your Comfort or 14-inch Medium.

Mattress fiberglass terms explained in plain English

Confused by terms like “fire sock,” “silica,” “URN,” or “CertiPUR-US” while checking your ComforPedic bed? Here's what each safety term on a tag or product page actually means.

Inner fire sock (FR sock)

It's a thin, stretchy mesh sleeve wrapped tightly around the foam core under the outer cover. It slows open flames, and it can shed glass shards if you unzip or tear the cover.

Assembly zipper vs. washable cover

A zipper on the bottom usually exists for factory assembly, not washing. Don't unzip it unless the care tag clearly says the outer cover is washable.

Glass fiber and core-spun glass

These are common tag names for fiberglass. Treat glass fiber, fiberglass, glass wool, core-spun glass, and glass yarn as the same woven glass material.

Silica-treated rayon vs. fiberglass

Silica-infused rayon chars safely like ash and doesn't shed sharp glass needles. Seeing silica-treated rayon alone isn't the same as woven fiberglass.

CertiPUR-US doesn't mean fiberglass-free

CertiPUR-US only tests the inner foam for VOCs and chemicals, never the fire sock. Only finished-product GOTS or GOLS organic standards ban fiberglass socks.

6-sided encasement vs. fitted protector

A fitted protector leaves the bottom seam open. Use a 6-sided zippered waterproof encasement if you need to seal a suspect bed completely.

URN and manufacture date

The Uniform Registry Number (like CA-XXXXX) and build date identify the factory and year. You'll need that date to check older pre-transition stock.

16 CFR Part 1632 vs. 1633

Every U.S. bed must pass both federal fire tests. Part 1632 tests smoldering cigarettes, while Part 1633 tests a 30-minute open flame.

QDoes ComforPedic use fiberglass in its mattresses?

QWhy isn't comforpedic.com online?

QHas ComforPedic ever been recalled for fiberglass?

No, ComforPedic has zero recalls in the U.S. CPSC recall list as of Nov 23, 2026. A check of the CPSC recall API on that same date also showed zero recalls.

QWhat is inside a ComforPedic mattress?

ComforPedic builds all-foam beds with gel or charcoal foam over firm base foam. The 12-inch Choose Your Comfort bed uses two foam layers over a 6-inch base, while the 14-inch Medium adds charcoal foam.
