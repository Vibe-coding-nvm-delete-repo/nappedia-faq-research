What fire barrier does Diamond Mattress use to meet federal rules?

Every bed sold in the U.S. must pass strict federal fire rules set by the CPSC. Under 16 C.F.R. part 1633, mattresses must resist open flames during a 30-minute test. Another rule, 16 C.F.R. part 1632, tests how a bed handles a lit cigarette.

To pass these tests without fiberglass, Diamond Mattress offers GOTS certified wool, organic cotton, and natural latex foam on select models. Skipping fiberglass helps prevent skin itch or dust issues if a cover is ever opened. The brand also uses high-density CertiPUR-US certified foam, which is tested for low emissions and no heavy metals.

Do Amazon, Costco, or third-party Diamond Mattress models differ?

Buying from a third-party store or clearance rack can sometimes mean getting older warehouse stock. While direct orders on diamondmattress.com are 100% fiberglass-free and include a 120-night sleep trial, older third-party units may have been built under past specs.

If you buy from Amazon, Costco, or a local shop, check the manufacture date on the permanent label. If the tag is missing or looks unclear, ask the seller for the exact build date before you sleep on it.

What exact language appears on a Diamond Mattress law tag?

Federal law requires every mattress to carry a permanent law tag showing the month, year, and plant location of manufacture. If a mattress pad uses a chemical flame retardant, it must also show the letter T and care steps. Please note that we have not photographed every individual tag in-house, so always read the label on your own bed.

  1. Find the white law tag sewn into the head or foot seam.
  2. Check the manufacture month and year to see if the bed is current stock.
  3. Read the fiber list for safe terms like wool, cotton, or rayon.
  4. Watch out for words like glass fiber or silica, which point to fiberglass.

Should you unzip or wash the Diamond Mattress mattress cover?

Even on a fiberglass-free bed, never unzip an inner fire-barrier sock or remove covers that are not meant to be washed. Opening a mattress cover can expose foam layers or disturb the fire barrier. Spot clean the surface gently, and use a washable mattress protector to keep your bed clean.

Safe to do
  • Use a washable mattress protector over the bed.
  • Spot clean small spills with mild soap and water.
  • Check the white law tag for care rules before cleaning.
Never do
  • Never unzip an inner fire-barrier sock or fixed cover.
  • Never machine-wash a mattress cover unless the tag allows it.
  • Never vacuum suspected fiberglass dust with a regular vacuum.

Step 1: Blot spills gently with mild soap and cold water

Dab the spot lightly with a damp microfiber cloth and mild dish soap. Never soak the fabric or scrub through the top quilt into the fire-barrier layer underneath.

Step 2: Air-dry the mattress flat without unzipping the cover

Leave the cover zipped and point a fan across the damp spot until fully dry. Machine-washing or tumble-drying a structural mattress cover can shrink it and expose the inner barrier.

Step 3: Add a breathable waterproof mattress protector

Put a washable waterproof protector over the intact cover so sweat, spills, and dust mites never reach the mattress fabric in the first place.

What should you do if you suspect fiberglass in a Diamond Mattress bed?

If you have an older bed and see shiny dust, do not unzip the cover any further. Shine a bright flashlight along the seams in a dark room to look for tiny, sparkling fibers. Do not use a standard vacuum, because it can blow glass dust into your home air.

Seal the entire mattress inside a zippered, waterproof encasement right away. Then contact Diamond Mattress customer support with your law-tag details so they can confirm your model's build.

Skin, Eye & Breathing Safety (When to See a Doctor)

Rinse exposed skin with cold water only, never hot water or scrubbing (hot water opens pores and traps fibers deeper). Seek medical care if you develop persistent coughing, wheezing, shortness of breath, eye pain, or a skin rash that does not clear after rinsing.

Step 1: Shut off fans and HVAC, close the door, and photograph proof

Turn off ceiling fans and central air right away so glass shards do not spread into ducts. Before touching the bed, take clear photos of the law tag and shiny dust for warranty support or a CPSC report.

Step 2: Put on disposable gloves, an N95 mask, and eye protection

Wear long sleeves, nitrile gloves, a fitted N95 respirator, and wraparound glasses before touching anything so fine fibers do not irritate your skin, eyes, or airways.

Step 3: Seal and double-bag the mattress before moving or disposing of it

Never drag an uncovered bed through hallways. Zip it inside a heavy waterproof vinyl encasement, tape the zipper seam shut, and double-bag in a heavy mattress disposal bag before hauling it to municipal waste.

Step 4: Vacuum slowly with a sealed True HEPA vacuum only

Never use a regular household vacuum, which blows glass fibers back through the exhaust. Use a sealed True HEPA vacuum on carpets, baseboards, bed frames, and soft furniture.

Step 5: Wet-wipe hard surfaces and replace HVAC air filters

Dry dusting pushes fibers back into the air. Wipe floors, nightstands, and windowsills with damp disposable microfiber cloths, then install fresh high-MERV HVAC filters.

Step 6: Wash lightly exposed clothes separately or discard heavily coated fabrics

Bag heavily coated sheets or curtains in sealed trash bags and discard them. For lightly exposed clothes, rinse outside first, wash alone on cold, and run two empty rinse cycles afterward.

QWhat certifications do Diamond Mattress foams have?

QDoes Diamond Mattress use natural materials?

Yes, the brand offers GOTS certified wool, organic cotton, and natural Talalay latex foam. Models like the Ethos Hybrid and Sage Organic Latex Hybrid use these materials.

QHas Diamond Mattress ever had a CPSC recall?

Mattresses must pass 16 C.F.R. part 1632 and 16 C.F.R. part 1633 fire tests. Diamond Mattress uses safe barriers like wool and organic cotton on select models to meet these rules.
