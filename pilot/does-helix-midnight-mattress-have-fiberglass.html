Which Helix Midnight models or years have fiberglass?

Current Helix Midnight models are likely fiberglass-free, but older ones may not be. Helix has stated that its mattresses are fiberglass-free, but that claim could not be verified from the Helix blog page, which returned a 'Not Found' error. For older models, check the law tag for the manufacture date. If the date is before Helix's transition to a fiberglass-free barrier, the mattress may contain fiberglass. Always check the law tag to be sure.

What fire barrier does Helix Midnight use?

Helix Midnight uses a rayon and cellulose fire barrier that is fiberglass-free. This barrier meets federal flammability standards. The table below compares the fire barrier types.

Barrier TypeContains Fiberglass?Common Use
Rayon/celluloseNoCurrent Helix Midnight
FiberglassYesSome older or budget mattresses
Chemical-treatedMay or may notSome mattress pads

Rayon and cellulose are common fiberglass-free options. They work by charring to slow flames. Federal law requires a fire barrier, but it does not require fiberglass.

How can you check your Helix Midnight law tag?

Look for the law tag sewn into the mattress edge or under the cover. The tag shows the month and year of manufacture and the factory location. Follow these steps: 1. Find the tag, often near the head or foot of the mattress. 2. Read the manufacture date. 3. Look for a 'T' label if the mattress pad was chemically treated. 4. Note the factory location. 5. Contact Helix support with the date and location to ask about fiberglass.

The law tag is your best clue for older models.

Should you unzip or wash the mattress cover?

No, do not unzip or wash the Helix Midnight cover. Unzipping can expose the fire barrier and release fibers. Washing may damage the barrier and reduce fire safety. Spot clean only with a damp cloth and mild soap. Keep the cover zipped and intact. If the cover is damaged, contact Helix for advice. Never remove the law tag.

What should you do if you suspect fiberglass?

Stop using the mattress and avoid disturbing the cover. Fiberglass fibers can irritate skin and lungs. Follow these steps: - Do not unzip or vacuum the mattress. - Cover the mattress with a sealed plastic bag. - Wear gloves and a mask if you must move it. - Contact Helix support for guidance. - Report the issue to the CPSC if you believe it is unsafe.

Keep the law tag for reference.

How do you pick a fiberglass-free mattress?

Ask the brand directly and check the law tag. Look for clear statements about fiberglass-free construction. Avoid mattresses with a 'T' label unless you confirm the treatment. Federal standards require fire resistance, but not fiberglass. Check for fiberglass-free claims on the brand's website or contact support. For more help, see our Mattress Fiberglass Safety Hub.

QWhat is a law tag and why does it matter?

A law tag is a permanent label required by federal rules. It shows the month and year of manufacture and the factory location. This helps you identify the model year and ask the brand about fiberglass.

QCan I remove the mattress cover to clean it?
