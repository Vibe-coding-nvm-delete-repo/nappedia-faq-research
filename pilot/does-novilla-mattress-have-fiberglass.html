Which Novilla models or years have fiberglass?

Current Novilla mattresses are fiberglass-free, though older stock may differ. Novilla removed fiberglass after a study suggested it might be a health threat. Today, Novilla states its mattresses do not contain fiberglass at all. Both hybrid and all-foam models use layered foam or coils to build the bed.

What fire barrier does Novilla use to meet federal rules?

Novilla does not publish one exact fire-barrier fiber across every model and year, so you must check the sewn-in law tag on your bed. Every mattress sold in the U.S. must pass strict federal fire tests. Novilla meets federal fire rules without using fiberglass in its current beds. The brand states its mattresses pass 16 CFR Part 1633 open-flame rules even without a foundation. Under CPSC mattress rules, Part 1633 tests open-flame safety for 30 minutes.

Novilla also states its beds pass 16 CFR Part 1632 smoldering tests. That rule checks if a bed resists a lighted cigarette with a char length of 2 inches or less. While Novilla states its foams are CertiPUR-US certified with low VOCs under 0.5 ppm, that foam test does not cover the outer cover or fire liner.

Do Amazon, Costco, or third-party Novilla models differ?

Buying from third-party sellers can sometimes mean getting older warehouse stock. Third-party listings can hold older stock that dates back before Novilla removed fiberglass. Novilla sells beds through its own site and major online stores like Amazon.

For example, a 2025 recall involved specific Novilla and Avenco beds made by PT Champion. Those beds sold on Amazon, Avencohome.com, and Novilla.net from January to April 2025 for $85 to $265. Always check the white side label and manufacture date when your bed arrives.

What exact language appears on a Novilla law tag?

On a Novilla law tag, check the fiber line for watch-out terms like Glass Fiber / Fiberglass or Silica / Glass Wool versus safe terms like Rayon / Cellulose Fiber or Polyester / Cotton. Federal law requires a permanent label on every mattress. Every mattress must show its manufacture date and maker location on this tag. Should you unzip or wash the Novilla mattress cover?

You should keep the mattress cover zipped on your bed. Never unzip an inner fire sock because it protects the foam and keeps fire-barrier fibers sealed. Even on fiberglass-free beds, removing the outer cover can damage the fire-resistant layer. Spot clean spills with mild soap and water instead of machine washing the cover.

Safe to do
  • Spot clean spills on the outer cover with mild soap and water.
  • Use a breathable, waterproof mattress protector over your Novilla bed.
  • Leave the sewn-in law tag attached so you can check the date.
Never do
  • Never unzip or remove an inner fire-barrier sock.
  • Never machine wash or dry-clean a non-removable mattress cover.
  • Never ignore a recalled PT Champion label from early 2025.

Step 1: Blot spills gently with mild soap and cold water

Dab the spot lightly with a damp microfiber cloth and mild dish soap. Never soak the fabric or scrub through the top quilt into the fire-barrier layer underneath.

Step 2: Air-dry the mattress flat without unzipping the cover

Leave the cover zipped and point a fan across the damp spot until fully dry. Machine-washing or tumble-drying a structural mattress cover can shrink it and expose the inner barrier.

Step 3: Add a breathable waterproof mattress protector

Put a washable waterproof protector over the intact cover so sweat, spills, and dust mites never reach the mattress fabric in the first place.

Has Novilla had any CPSC recalls or fiberglass complaints?

Novilla has faced a federal fire-safety recall, though it was not for fiberglass. CPSC Recall #26062 recalled 5,340 beds made by PT Champion on October 30, 2025. Those Avenco and Novilla beds failed 16 CFR 1633 fire rules. Affected units have a white side label listing PT Champion and dates from December 2024 to January 9, 2025.

Models include Novilla N-M02043-12-F-2, Avenco/Novilla B-M02901-14-Q-1, and B-M02905-6-T. Owners should stop using recalled beds and email recall@champmattress.com or visit novilla.net/pages/recall for a free zippered fire cover. Public records show no settled nationwide class-action judgment against Novilla International LLC, though 12 BBB complaints in 3 years note customer-service friction.

What should you do if you suspect fiberglass in a Novilla bed?

If you own an older Novilla bed and see shiny dust, do not panic. Do not use a standard vacuum because it can blow tiny fibers into your room air. Turn off your HVAC fan and use a bright flashlight to check the mattress surface and nearby floor.

Seal the mattress inside a heavy-duty zippered encasement right away. Then contact Novilla customer support with your law tag details to confirm your model's build.

Skin, Eye & Breathing Safety (When to See a Doctor)

Rinse exposed skin with cold water only, never hot water or scrubbing (hot water opens pores and traps fibers deeper). Seek medical care if you develop persistent coughing, wheezing, shortness of breath, eye pain, or a skin rash that does not clear after rinsing.

Step 1: Shut off fans and HVAC, close the door, and photograph proof

Turn off ceiling fans and central air right away so glass shards do not spread into ducts. Before touching the bed, take clear photos of the law tag and shiny dust for warranty support or a CPSC report.

Step 2: Put on disposable gloves, an N95 mask, and eye protection

Wear long sleeves, nitrile gloves, a fitted N95 respirator, and wraparound glasses before touching anything so fine fibers do not irritate your skin, eyes, or airways.

Step 3: Seal and double-bag the mattress before moving or disposing of it

Never drag an uncovered bed through hallways. Zip it inside a heavy waterproof vinyl encasement, tape the zipper seam shut, and double-bag in a heavy mattress disposal bag before hauling it to municipal waste.

Step 4: Vacuum slowly with a sealed True HEPA vacuum only

Never use a regular household vacuum, which blows glass fibers back through the exhaust. Use a sealed True HEPA vacuum on carpets, baseboards, bed frames, and soft furniture.

Step 5: Wet-wipe hard surfaces and replace HVAC air filters

Dry dusting pushes fibers back into the air. Wipe floors, nightstands, and windowsills with damp disposable microfiber cloths, then install fresh high-MERV HVAC filters.

Step 6: Wash lightly exposed clothes separately or discard heavily coated fabrics

Bag heavily coated sheets or curtains in sealed trash bags and discard them. For lightly exposed clothes, rinse outside first, wash alone on cold, and run two empty rinse cycles afterward.

Mattress fiberglass terms explained in plain English

Confused by terms like “fire sock,” “silica,” “URN,” or “CertiPUR-US” while checking your Novilla mattress? Here is what each safety term on a mattress tag or product page actually means.

Inner Fire Sock (FR Sock)

A thin, stretchy mesh sleeve pulled tightly around the foam core underneath the outer cover. It slows open flames, and can shed tiny glass shards if the outer cover is unzipped or torn.

Assembly Zipper vs. Washable Cover

A zipper on the bottom of a mattress usually exists only for factory assembly, not washing. Never unzip it unless the care tag clearly says the outer cover is removable and washable.

Glass Fiber / Core-Spun Glass

Common U.S. law-tag names for fiberglass. Treat 'glass fiber,' 'fiberglass,' 'glass wool,' 'core-spun glass,' and 'textured glass yarn' as the same woven glass barrier.

Silica-Treated Rayon vs. Fiberglass

Silica-infused rayon or viscose chars safely like ash and does not shed sharp glass needles. Seeing 'silica-treated rayon' alone is not the same as woven glass fiber.

CertiPUR-US Does Not Mean Fiberglass-Free

CertiPUR-US only tests the foam inside the bed for VOCs and chemicals, never the outer cover or fire sock. Only finished-product GOTS or GOLS organic standards ban fiberglass socks.

6-Sided Encasement vs. Fitted Protector

A fitted protector only covers the top and sides, leaving the bottom seam open. Use a 6-sided zippered waterproof encasement if you want to seal a suspect mattress completely.

URN & Manufacture Date

The Uniform Registry Number (like CA-XXXXX) and build date on the white law tag identify the exact factory and year. Use that date to check pre-transition or older stock.

16 CFR Part 1632 vs. 1633

Two federal fire rules every U.S. bed must pass: 1632 tests smoldering cigarettes, while 1633 tests a 30-minute open flame (which pushed budget foam brands to use fire socks).

QDo current Novilla mattresses contain fiberglass?

Novilla states its current mattresses do not contain fiberglass at all. The brand removed fiberglass after a study suggested the material might be a health threat.

QCould an older Novilla mattress still have fiberglass?

Older stock made before Novilla removed fiberglass may differ from current models. Always check the manufacture date on your mattress law tag to be sure.

QDoes CertiPUR-US prove a Novilla bed has no fiberglass?

No, CertiPUR-US only tests the foam for low VOCs and harmful chemicals. It does not test the outer cover or the fire-barrier liner.

QWas the 2025 Novilla recall about fiberglass?

No, CPSC Recall #26062 recalled 5,340 Avenco and Novilla beds for failing 16 CFR 1633 fire rules. Affected beds have a white side label listing PT Champion.

QHow do I know if my Novilla bed is part of the recall?

Check the white side label for PT Champion and dates from December 2024 to January 9, 2025. Affected models include N-M02043-12-F-2, B-M02901-14-Q-1, and B-M02905-6-T.

QWhat should I do if my Novilla bed is recalled?
