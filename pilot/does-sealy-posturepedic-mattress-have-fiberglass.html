Does Sealy Posturepedic mattress have fiberglass?

No, Sealy Posturepedic mattresses do not contain fiberglass. Learn about the flame barrier, how to check your law tag, and what to do if you suspect fiberglass.

Which Sealy Posturepedic models or years have fiberglass?

No current Sealy Posturepedic model uses fiberglass. Sealy says its mattresses do not contain any form of fiberglass. This covers Posturepedic Innerspring, Hybrid, and Plus models. Older Posturepedic beds may have used different materials, but Sealy's current statement applies to all its mattresses. For the full brand picture, see the Sealy fiberglass guide.

What fire barrier does Sealy Posturepedic use?

Sealy Posturepedic uses a rayon and polyester blend as its flame barrier. Sealy states the flame barrier in every Sealy mattress is a rayon and polyester blend. For hybrid models, Sealy says the mattresses are made without chemical flame retardants. The table below compares Sealy product types.

Product typeFlame barrierFiberglass?
Sealy Posturepedic (all tiers)Rayon and polyester blendNo
Cocoon by SealyRayon and polyester blendNo

Sealy's flame barrier helps the mattress pass fire-resistance tests and meet federal, state, and local fire rules, including the National Flammability Standard.

How can you check your Sealy Posturepedic law tag?

Check the law tag sewn into your mattress for fiberglass details. The tag is required by federal rules and lists the materials used. Follow these steps:

  1. Find the law tag, usually at the head or foot of the mattress.
  2. Look for the fiber content list. It should say rayon and polyester, not fiberglass.
  3. Check for a "T" label if the mattress pad has a chemical flame retardant.
  4. Note the manufacture date and location, which must be on the tag.
  5. If the tag is missing or unclear, contact Sealy customer service with your model number.

Sealy directs readers to the Consumer Product Safety Commission at www.cpsc.gov for more details on fire safety rules.

Should you unzip or wash the mattress cover?

No, do not unzip or wash the Sealy Posturepedic mattress cover. The cover is not designed to be removed for cleaning. Unzipping it can expose inner materials and may damage the fire barrier. For hybrid models, Sealy uses a rayon and polyester blend barrier that should stay in place. Spot clean only with a damp cloth and mild soap. If the cover is removable, follow the care label exactly.

What should you do if you suspect fiberglass?

Stop using the mattress and check the law tag first. If you see shiny fibers or have skin irritation, take these steps:

  • Do not shake or vacuum the mattress, as this can spread fibers.
  • Check the law tag for fiberglass content.
  • Contact Sealy customer support with your model and date of manufacture.
  • If fibers are loose, cover the mattress with a fitted sheet and keep pets and children away.
  • Report any skin irritation to a doctor.

Sealy says some manufacturers use exposed, brittle fiberglass that can crumble and irritate skin. Sealy states its own mattresses do not contain any form of fiberglass.

How do you pick a fiberglass-free mattress?

Look for a brand that states its mattresses contain no fiberglass. Sealy says its mattresses do not contain any form of fiberglass. Check the law tag and ask the seller for written confirmation. Avoid mattresses with exposed glass fiber in the cover. For other brands, see our guides on Novaform and Avocado. Also visit the Mattress Fiberglass Safety Hub for more tips.

More FAQs about Sealy Posturepedic fiberglass (3)

QDoes Sealy Posturepedic Hybrid have fiberglass?

No, Sealy Posturepedic Hybrid mattresses do not contain fiberglass. Sealy states its mattresses do not contain any form of fiberglass. Hybrid models use a rayon and polyester blend flame barrier and are made without chemical flame retardants.

QWhat is core-spun glass fiber in Sealy mattresses?

Core-spun glass fiber is a type of glass fiber encased in a protective sheath. Sealy uses it only in the fire sock of some foam-only models, never in the foam or cover. It stays fully enclosed during normal use.

QAre Sealy mattresses safe under federal fire rules?

Yes, Sealy states all its products comply with applicable laws, including CPSC rules. The CPSC sets mattress flammability standards to reduce fire deaths and injuries. Sealy conducts regular independent testing to ensure compliance.
