1. Live Multi-Engine Search Harvest: Every Topic is probed across Google & Bing autocomplete (with automatic Bing latex-filter bypass) + SERP discovery to verify real US search demand.

2. Zero-Drop Reconciliation & Zero Similarity Drops: Deterministic code removes only exact duplicates and hard-banned brand/non-US/accessory junk. Zero candidates are silently dropped or killed by cosine similarity.

3. Cross-Corpus & Cross-Category Deduplication: Real LLM Semantic Judges compare every candidate against all 322 built/drafted Nappedia pages (corpus.json) PLUS all previously completed categories so zero cannibalization survives.

4. Primary-Source Citation Verification: Clinical claims link only to NCBI E-utilities verified PubMed PMIDs, federal CFR safety rules (16 CFR 1632/1633/1213), ASTM standards, and audited test labs/forums.

5. Split Dual-Critic 100% Sweep: Two independent critics audit 100% of kept pages AND 100% of rejected/merged/covered rows until both certify VERDICT: PASS (>= 9.5/10, 0 Critical, 0 Major).

6. Hierarchy: Every piece of research follows one consistent structure: Category → Topic → Page.
